About me

The deeper I looked into commerce and information systems, the less satisfied I was with textbook theory alone – I needed to learn about what really drives the mechanics behind contemporary business processes.

What I have learned is that finance and technology are not as separate as people think. Finance, like accounting, teaches you to be careful and think clearly which is necessary for understanding how businesses work. Technology and information systems give you the tools to analyze and improve a business. If you do not have one or the other, you do not have the full picture.

By studying finance and accounting, I am learning how to ask questions, think carefully about the systems in place, and understand the core commercial processes behind them.
